Tenses: Time and Aspect

Tense conveys two primary dimensions of an action: Time (when it occurs) and Aspect (the nature or "type" of the action).

Verbal Aspects

AoristFocuses on the action as a whole or a simple event; "undefined."aor.
PerfectiveFocuses on a completed action with continuing results/state.perf.

Identify Verbs in the Septuagint

Greek verbs are built like modular code: Prefix (Augment) + Root + Tense Marker + Personal Ending.

Primary Endings (Present & Future)

Used for the Present and Future tenses. These indicate actions in the "now" or "ahead" systems.
EndingPerson / NumberMeaning
-ω1st Sing.Present/Future Active: "I [verb]"
-εις2nd Sing.Present/Future Active: "You [verb]"
-ει3rd Sing.Present/Future Active: "He/She/It [verb]"
-ομεν1st Plur.Present/Future Active: "We [verb]"
-ετε2nd Plur.Present/Future Active: "You all [verb]"
-ουσι(ν)3rd Plur.Present/Future Active: "They [verb]"
-σωFuture MarkerThe "sigma" (σ) inserted before the ending indicates the Future tense.

Secondary Endings (Imperfect & Aorist)

Used for the Past systems. These are almost always accompanied by an Augment (an ε- prefix).
EndingPerson / NumberMeaning
-ον1st Sing. / 3rd Plur.Imperfect/Aorist Active: "I [verb]" or "They [verb]"
-ες2nd Sing.Imperfect/Aorist Active: "You [verb]"
-ε(ν)3rd Sing.Imperfect/Aorist Active: "He/She/It [verb]"
-ομεν1st Plur.Imperfect/Aorist Active: "We [verb]"
-ετε2nd Plur.Imperfect/Aorist Active: "You all [verb]"
-σαAorist MarkerThe "sigma-alpha" (σα) suffix indicates the First Aorist (completed past action).

The "Augment" & Infinitives

These are the structural "flags" that help you categorize the verb type immediately.
EndingTypeMeaning
ε- (Prefix)The AugmentA systemic flag placed before the root indicating a Past Tense (Imperfect, Aorist, or Pluperfect).
-εινPresent InfinitiveThe non-finite form: "To [verb]" (e.g., akouein - to hear).
-σαιAorist InfinitiveThe non-finite form for a completed action: "To [verb]" (viewed as a whole).

Parsing Algorithm: Verbs in the Septuagint

When you see a verb in the Septuagint, apply this algorithm:

  • Check for an ε- prefix: If yes, it is a Past Tense (Secondary).
  • Check for a σ before the ending: If yes, it is either Future (-σο) or Aorist (-σα).
  • Check the ending: Use the tables above to find the Person (Who) and Number (How many).

Logic & Functional Definition

1. Perspective (Stative Aspect)
: Focuses on the existing state resulting from a completed action. It is not just that the action is "done," but that the "doneness" defines the current subject.

  • Perfect: Present state resulting from past action (e.g., "I have seen" = "I am in a state of knowing").
  • Pluperfect: Past state resulting from a previous action (e.g., "I had seen").
  • Future Perfect: A state that will exist in the future (e.g., "I will have seen").

2. Aorist (Aorist Aspect)
: Derived from a-oristos ("without boundaries"). It treats the action as a single point or "snapshot."

  • Aorist: The "simple past." It ignores duration or internal complexity. It simply states that the event occurred.

3. Imperfective (Continuous Aspect)
: Focuses on the process, duration, or repetition of the action.

  • Present: Action happening now, viewed as an ongoing process.
  • Imperfect: Action happening in the past, viewed as an ongoing process (e.g., "I was writing" vs. the aorist "I wrote").
