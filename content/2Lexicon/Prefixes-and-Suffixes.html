> **Privative** describes an adjective or noun that indicates the absence, loss, or negation of a quality (e.g., "un-" in unwise or "-less" in harmless), stemming from Latin to mean "depriving". It signifies a lack of something rather than a positive existence. Synonyms include negative, deprived, bereft, and destitute. --- ### -ιος *Adjective (Characteristic / Belonging)* *-al / -ous / -y* > ### Notes > > > * Glorious (characterized by glory) > * Primary (of prime) > >
