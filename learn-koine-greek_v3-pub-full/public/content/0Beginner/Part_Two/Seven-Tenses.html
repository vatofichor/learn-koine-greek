The Seven Tenses Overview

Present Tense (pres.)

Action occurring in the present; usually imperfective aspect.

x is happening...

Imperfect Tense (imperf.)

Continuous, repeated, or attempted action occurring in the past.

x kept happening before.

Future Tense (fut.)

Action that will occur in the future; aspect is typically undefined.

x will happen.

Aorist Tense (aor.)

Simple past action viewed as a single point in time.

### Notes

Describes a completed, punctual, or "snapshot" action; typically occurring in the past without indicating its


duration or internal details.
It focuses on the action as a whole rather than its ongoing process.

Perfect Tense (perf.)

A present state resulting from an action completed in the past.

x happened; so this is how y is now.

Pluperfect Tense (plup.)

A past state resulting from an action completed in the remote past.

y had become as; because x happened.

Future Perfect Tense (fut. perf.)

A state that will exist in the future following a completed action.

this is how y will be; after x happens.
