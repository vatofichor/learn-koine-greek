Parts of Speech Overview

Noun (n.)

A word representing a person, place, thing, or idea.

Adjective (adj.)

A word that modifies or describes a noun or pronoun.

Adverb (adv.)

A word that modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

Verbal Adjective (v. adj.)

An adjective derived from a verb.

Conjunction (conj.)

A word used to connect clauses or sentences.

Pronoun (pro.)

A word that *takes the place of a specific noun.

Preposition (prep.)

A word showing the relationship of a noun to another word.

Reciprocal Pronoun (recip. pro.)

A pronoun indicating a mutual action or relationship (e.g., "each other").

### Notes

each other, one another indicate a mutual relationship, where two or more subjects perform the same action on


each other. They function only as objects, never subjects.
Use each other for two people/things, and one another for three or more.

Relative Pronoun (rel. pro.)

A pronoun that introduces a subordinate / relative clause.

### Notes

who, whom, whose, which, that
connect dependent clauses to independent clauses, acting as subjects or objects to modify nouns (antecedents).
They specify, identify, or add extra information*, essential for creating complex sentences.
