# Verbal Moods and Verbal Nouns/Adjectives Overview

    ## INDICATIVE

    Expresses a **statement of fact or reality.** *ind.*

    ---

    ## INFINITIVE

    A *verbal noun* expressing the *action of the verb without person or number*. *inf.*

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > * to be
    > * to have
    > * to do
    > * to say
    > * to go
    > * to get
    > * to make
    > * to know
    > * to think
    > * to take
    > * to see
    > * to come
    > * to want
    > * to look
    > * to use
    >
    >

    ---

    ## IMPERATIVE

    Expresses a **direct command, request, or prohibition**. *impv.*

    ---

    ## OPTATIVE

    Expresses a wish, a potentiality, or a softened command. *opt.*

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > * **Wishes/Prayers**: "May the best team win!" or "God help us!"
    > * **Hypothetical Situations**: "If only we could stop the battle"
    > * **Purpose/Fear Clauses**: Expressing "so that it could happen" or "for fear that it might happen" in past
    contexts
    > * **Greetings**: "Farewell" or blessings
    > * **Potentiality**: "We could/might be stopping the battle" (often with particles in other languages)
    >
    >

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > The optative expresses **desires, hopes, or potentiality** ("would," "should," "let it be").

    ---

    ## PARTICIPLE

    A **verbal adjective** that shares characteristics of both verbs and adjectives. | ptcp.

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > "-ing," "-ed," "-en," "-t"

    **Verbal adjective** is a word derived from a verb that functions as an adjective to modify a noun, typically taking
    the form of a present participle (-ing) or past participle (-ed, -en).

    * **Usage**: They can appear before the noun they modify (the boring movie) or after a linking verb (the movie was
    boring).
    * **Deverbal Adjectives**: Some forms no longer act like verbs and function exclusively as adjectives (e.g., a
    drunken sailor).
    * *Present Participle:* "The crying baby" (describes the baby).
    * *Past Participle:* "The broken vase" (describes the vase).

    ---

    ## SUBJUNCTIVE

    Expresses **probability, desire, or hypothetical actions**. *subj.*

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > * **Hypothetical/Contrary-to-Fact**: "If I were to win the lottery, I would buy a boat".
    > * **Wishes/Desires**: "I wish she were here".
    > * **Suggestions/Demands** (Mandative): "I suggest that he study more".
    > * **Set Phrases**: "Come what may," "If need be," "God save the Queen".
    >
    >

    > ### Notes
    >
    >
    > Subjunctive expresses **probability, intention, or contingency** ("might," "may"); *acting as a "weakened"
    future*.
