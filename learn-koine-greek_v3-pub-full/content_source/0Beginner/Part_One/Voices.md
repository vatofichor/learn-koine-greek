# Verbal Voices Overview

    ## Active Voice

    The **subject performs the action** of the verb. *act.*

    ---

    ## Middle Voice

    The *subject* performs the action **upon themselves or in their own interest**. *mid.*

    ---

    ## Passive Voice

    The **subject is the recipient of the action** performed by another. *pass.*
