    ### -λιτος

    *Adjective (Privative)*

    *-less / without / un-*

    > * Shameless
    > * Unbound
    > * Harmless
    > * Unfettered
    > * Weightless

    > **Privative** describes an adjective or noun that indicates the absence, loss, or negation of a quality (e.g.,
    > "un-" in unwise or "-less" in harmless), stemming from Latin to mean "depriving". It signifies a lack of something
    > rather than a positive existence. Synonyms include negative, deprived, bereft, and destitute.
