Verbal Aspects Overview

Imperfective Aspect

impf.

Focuses on the process, duration, or ongoing nature of the action.

How long it's done, how it's done, and done repeatedly.

Aorist Aspect

(aor.)

Focuses on the action as a whole, a simple event, or a "snapshot".

x occurred.

Perfective Aspect

perf.

Focuses on a completed action with continuing results or a present state.

x happened; so y is this way or is happening as a result.
