Greek Cases Overview

⭐ Nominative

The subject or predicate nominative. | nom.

🔗 Genitive

"description, origin, or separation; indicates possession. gen.

Typically using an apostrophe + 's' or "of"; that indicates a relationship between nouns, most commonly possession, description, or origin.

📦 Dative

"the indirect object, location, or means. dat.

Primarily indicates the indirect object; the recipient or beneficiary of an action.

🎯 Accusative

"the *direct object or extent of space/time affected. acc.

Direct object of a transitive verb; the person or thing directly affected by the action.

📣 Vocative

"used for direct address or invoking. voc."

Comparison Matrix

Nominative [Subject]
: Who/What performs the action?

Genitive [Possession]
: Whose is it? / From where (source)?

Dative [Indirect]
: To/For whom? / By what means (instrument)?

Accusative [Direct Obj]
: Who/What does the action affect?

Vocative [Address]
: Calling or addressing* the entity directly.
