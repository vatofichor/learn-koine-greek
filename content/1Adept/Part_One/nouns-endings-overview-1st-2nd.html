# 1st and 2nd Declension Case Endings Overview ## Nominative * **singular:** -η, -α, -ης, -ος, -ον * **plural:** -αι, -οι, -α --- ## Genitive * **singular:** -ης, -ας, -ου * **plural:** -ων --- ## Dative * **singular:** -ῃ, -ᾳ, -ῳ * **plural:** -αις, -οις --- ## Accusative * **singular:** -ην, -αν, -ον * **plural:** -ας, -ους, -α --- ## Vocative * **singular:** -η, -α, -ε, -ον * **plural:** -αι, -οι, -α
